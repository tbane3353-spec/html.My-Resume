My Hobbies

Outside of work, I enjoy exploring a wide range of creative and active hobbies that keep me inspired, grounded, and balanced. I believe that personal growth comes from curiosity and passion — and my free time reflects exactly that.

Music plays a big part in my life. I love DJing and mixing different genres, especially funky disco house with touches of pop and R&B. I also play guitar, which helps me unwind and express myself through sound and rhythm.

Camping and spending time in nature recharge me. Whether it’s hiking, exploring new landscapes, or just sitting by a campfire, I find peace and perspective outdoors. It’s also a great way to reset my creativity and come back with fresh ideas.

Staying active is important to me. I practice calisthenics and bodyweight training at home — focusing on strength, flexibility, and consistency. I also enjoy walking my dog, which combines exercise and relaxation perfectly.

Photography is one of my favorite ways to capture stories and emotions through visuals. I’m drawn to vibrant lighting, natural landscapes, and moments that tell a story without words. It’s a hobby that ties into my eye for detail, something I also apply in my professional work.
